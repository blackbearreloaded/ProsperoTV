## More channels play

- Interlaced channels (broadcast 1080i) now play, with their two fields blended so motion is smooth.
- 4K HEVC channels signalled above level 5.1, as satellite 4K channels are, now open.
- Chinese, Japanese and Korean channel names and categories are shown as written.
- A source may hold up to 250,000 channels, and its list is read as it downloads.

## New

- A phone remote: Settings, Pair a phone. Scan the code, enter the number shown, and the phone's browser moves the menu, searches and sets the volume.
- A diagnostic log: Settings, Troubleshooting. Off by default; while it is on the app records what it does in logs/debug-trace.txt, without account details, to send with a problem report.
- A volume setting.

## Fixes

- A channel that does not play says why, in the menu and in the log.
- The release ZIP stores every file open to all, so a copy that keeps permissions still starts.

Note: this is the first update ProsperoTV installs by itself. Choose Update now and the app downloads it, closes and comes back as 01.000.030; your sources, favorites and settings stay as they are.

## Installing by hand

- `PPSA99003.zip`: extract it and upload the complete `PPSA99003` folder to `/data/homebrew`, replacing the old one.
- `SHA256SUMS`: the checksum of the download.
- Coming from 01.000.015 or older: close ProsperoTV, delete `PPSA99003.ffpfsc` from `/data/homebrew` if you used the image, then upload the folder and restart ShadowMountPlus or the console.

This release was built by this repository's GitHub Actions from the tagged commit.
