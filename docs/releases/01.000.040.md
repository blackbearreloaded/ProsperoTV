## Picture and sound

- The sound no longer runs ahead of the picture. It was ahead by about a second on most channels and two on 4K ones; the picture now follows the sound's clock.
- 4K films and episodes play without stopping. They used to freeze for eight seconds every few seconds.
- A channel that never answers no longer holds the buttons: next, previous and back work while it is still opening.

## New

- Movies and TV shows from your provider, in the On demand tab, with audio tracks and subtitles.
- A programme guide: now and next beside each channel, the full grid on R3, catch-up where the provider keeps an archive.
- Several sources at once: more playlists and accounts, MAC-code portals, HDHomeRun and Tvheadend.
- Your provider's own categories, as the row of lists in Live TV and with subcategories on Touchpad. Hide the ones you do not want.
- While watching: up and down change channel, Cross opens the channel list, Square returns to the channel before, Options chooses audio and subtitles.
- Pause live TV and go back a few minutes.
- Two or four channels at once (L3).
- Channel logos, and a live preview with sound in the large television.
- Favorite folders, a parental PIN with a kids-only mode, and a sleep timer.
- Sources can be added and edited from the phone remote.
- Each console user has their own sources, favorites and settings.
- Menus in Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese and Dutch, following the console's language.
- HDR10 and HLG channels are shown as HDR.
- Back up and restore your settings on a USB drive.

## Fixes

- Category names written in raised letters read normally, and names with a slash ("24/7") are no longer split in two.
- Interlaced channels keep their full motion.
- Holding L2 or R2 in Movies and TV shows keeps turning pages, faster the longer it is held.
- With no USB drive connected, backup says so.

## Installing by hand

- `PPSA99003.zip`: extract it and upload the complete `PPSA99003` folder to `/data/homebrew`, replacing the old one.
- `SHA256SUMS`: the checksum of the download.
- Coming from 01.000.015 or older: close ProsperoTV, delete `PPSA99003.ffpfsc` from `/data/homebrew` if you used the image, then upload the folder and restart ShadowMountPlus or the console.

This release was built by this repository's GitHub Actions from the tagged commit.
