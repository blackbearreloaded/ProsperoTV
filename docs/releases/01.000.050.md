## Fixes

- Live channels whose playlist lists hours of broadcast now open. They used to fail with "HLS playlist is not playable: output limit" (Jordan TV in the built-in list, for one).
- Live channels that date each segment in their playlist now open. They used to fail with "malformed playlist".

## New

- **Normalize channel volume**, in Settings, off by default: brings quiet and loud channels to a similar level. Contributed by JMUtechnologies.

## Installing by hand

- `PPSA99003.zip`: extract it and upload the complete `PPSA99003` folder to `/data/homebrew`, replacing the old one.
- `SHA256SUMS`: the checksum of the download.
- Coming from 01.000.015 or older: close ProsperoTV, delete `PPSA99003.ffpfsc` from `/data/homebrew` if you used the image, then upload the folder and restart ShadowMountPlus or the console.

This release was built by this repository's GitHub Actions from the tagged commit.
